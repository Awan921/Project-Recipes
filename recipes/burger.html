Burger Recipe

Description

A Burger is a popular fast-food dish made with a soft bun, a cooked patty, fresh vegetables, cheese, and sauces.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Beef patty
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise
    •

Steps

  1. Cook the beef patty until fully done.
  2. Toast the burger buns.
  3. Place cheese on the hot patty.
  4. Add lettuce, tomato, and onion.
  5. Add ketchup and mayonnaise.
  6. Cover with the top bun and serve.
