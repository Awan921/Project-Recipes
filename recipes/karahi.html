karahi Recipe

Description

Karahi is a popular Pakistani dish made with tender meat, tomatoes, green chilies, ginger, garlic, and traditional spices cooked together in a karahi.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chilies
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Red chili powder
  • Garam masala
    •

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a karahi and cook the chicken until lightly browned.
  2. Add ginger and garlic and fry for a few minutes.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  4. Add red chili powder and other spices.
  5. Cook until the chicken is fully tender and the oil separates.
  6. Add green chilies and garnish with fresh ginger.
  7. Serve hot with naan or roti.
