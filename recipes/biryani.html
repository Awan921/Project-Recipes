Biryani Recipe

Description

Biryani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with basmati rice, spices, and tender meat.

It is a popular South Asian dish known for its rich taste and delicious aroma.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Chicken
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Yogurt
  • Spices
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Green chilies
  • Coriander
    •

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the rice.
  2. Cook the chicken with onions, tomatoes, yogurt, and spices.
  3. Boil the rice until it is almost cooked.
  4. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat for 15 to 20 minutes.
  6. Mix gently and serve hot.
